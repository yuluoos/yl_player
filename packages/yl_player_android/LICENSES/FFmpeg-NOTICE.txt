FFmpeg 9.0.1 is used under the GNU Lesser General Public License,
version 2.1 or later. The packaged Android bridge enables no GPL or nonfree
components, no encoders, no filters, and no FFmpeg network protocols.

The complete bridge source is android/src/main/cpp/yl_player_ffmpeg.cpp.
The pinned, signature-verifying build and relink procedure is in
tool/android_ffmpeg/build_shared_libraries.sh. It produces one dynamically
loaded and replaceable libyl_player_ffmpeg.so for each supported Android ABI.

Upstream source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.1.tar.xz
Corresponding source and local modifications: this package and the build
script above.
